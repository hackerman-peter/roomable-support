Help for landlords using Roomable to organise rooms, tenants, rent, utilities, bonds and signed agreements.

Need help?

Email us with what you were trying to do, what happened, and your iPhone model. Please do not email tenancy agreements, passwords or other sensitive tenant information.

Email Roomable support

Signing in

You can use Google, Apple, or an email sign-in link. Signing in creates your own empty workspace. It does not reveal another person’s homes.

Sharing a property

A full owner can open Settings, choose a property, and invite a family member. Each person sees only the properties shared with their account.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Family access → Delete account. Roomable shows what will be removed before asking for final confirmation. Shared homes stay with their other owners; a home with no other owner and its Roomable records will be deleted.
