Room records

Every room, on one screen you can trust.

Today lists what is overdue, due today and coming up across every property. Each tenant’s page shows how far rent is paid through, the next due date and how long is left on the agreement.

  • ✓Tap the circle when rent arrives, or record a partial amount.
  • ✓Paid-through dates and tenancy end reminders, per room.
  • ✓Agreement dates and days remaining on every tenancy.

Family collaboration

The whole family, each in their own language.

Share a property by invite code. Each person opens Roomable in English or 简体中文 and sees the same rooms, the same rent and the same bills.

  • ✓Roles per property: full owner, manager, or view only.
  • ✓Shared records refresh after a change is saved.
  • ✓Same figures for everyone; language is a personal setting.

Receipts and documents

A numbered receipt for every dollar you record.

Each recorded payment gets a receipt with the premises, room, method, purpose and date. Corrections keep the original entry. The signed agreement, bond facts and dates live under the tenancy, where you will find them.

  • ✓Receipts numbered per property, like RIV-0003.
  • ✓Store the agreement you signed elsewhere. Roomable does not e-sign.
  • ✓Utility bills keep each tenant’s share until it is recorded.

Three steps. No bank connection, no setup call.

01

Add your properties and rooms

Each house, each room, each tenant and their rent. Existing tenancies start from a date you choose.

02

Record rent when it arrives

Tap the circle for a full period, or record the exact amount received. A receipt is written each time.

03

Split bills and share with family

Enter a bill, choose who pays, split equally or by amount. Invite the family so everyone sees the same thing.

Our story

It started with our family in Perth.

Roomable began with a Perth family managing rental properties together. The records lived across paper, spreadsheets and messages, and not everyone in the family preferred the same language.

We wanted one calm place where each room’s rent and shared bills were clear to everyone who needed to see them, in English or 简体中文. That is why we built Roomable.
